Our community

More than business cards.

Our community brings together young professionals who want to exchange ideas and build meaningful connections across the energy transition.

Informal gatherings, lasting connections

From rooftop drinks at Zoku to picnics, canal boat outings and conversations with guest speakers, EYP gatherings create opportunities to meet peers in a relaxed setting. Returning regularly helps turn introductions into familiar faces and lasting relationships.

Contribute to the community

Bring your questions, share your experience and take an interest in other members' work. You can also connect us with potential speakers, hosts or sponsors through Partnerships.

Membership fees

As a thank-you for joining during our first two years, members who join by December 31st 2026 pay a one-off €7 membership fee. Members joining from January 1st 2027 pay an annual fee, initially €10 per year. Existing members keep their original membership terms.

Membership contributions help support speakers, the website, communication and community activities.
