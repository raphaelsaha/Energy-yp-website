Our purpose

A community built on connection

Energy Young Professionals connects people working across energy, sustainability and mobility. Our Amsterdam community makes room for conversations across disciplines, from renewables and grids to finance, consulting and clean tech.

Meet, exchange and stay connected

We meet in person each month to share experiences, hear different perspectives and get to know the people shaping the energy transition. Informal gatherings make it easier to continue conversations and build lasting professional relationships.

Explore EYP

Learn about our story, discover our gatherings, or read how to join. Organizations interested in speaking, hosting or sponsorship can visit Partnerships.
