Joining EYP

Find your people in energy.

We welcome people who want to contribute to a friendly, engaged community and attend regularly.

Who the community is for

  • Young professionals under 35.
  • People based in or near Amsterdam.
  • People working or studying in energy, sustainability, mobility or related fields.
  • Members interested in taking part in recurring gatherings.

Get connected

Join our WhatsApp group to connect with the community and hear about upcoming events. Register for individual gatherings through the Luma link in Upcoming events.

Join our WhatsApp group ↗

Membership fees

As a thank-you for joining during our first two years, members who join by December 31st 2026 pay a one-off €7 membership fee. Members joining from January 1st 2027 pay an annual fee, initially €10 per year. Existing members keep their original membership terms.

For the €7 one-time membership payment, use the payment QR code on the homepage.
