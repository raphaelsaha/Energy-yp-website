Events

See you at the next gathering.

We organize informal networking evenings, guest-speaker sessions and occasional social activities in Amsterdam. Our regular gatherings take place on the last Thursday of the month.

What an evening looks like

Speaker evenings combine a short industry presentation with questions and informal networking. Topics can include energy markets, infrastructure, innovation, climate tech, financing and the practical challenges of the energy transition. Social gatherings offer another way to get to know the community.
