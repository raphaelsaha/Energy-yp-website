About EYP

Good conversations. Stronger connections.

Where we started

Energy Young Professionals began in Hamburg, Germany, in summer 2023. That community has grown to more than 200 people. The network expanded to Amsterdam in November 2024, where it has grown organically to around 70 members.

A network across the energy transition

Our members work across renewables, grids, finance, consulting, clean tech, mobility and related fields. Meeting people outside our everyday teams gives us a broader view of the sector and its practical challenges.

What brings us together

We aim to build a welcoming, engaged network where people know one another. Regular gatherings create space to exchange ideas, learn from experienced speakers and develop long-term professional relationships.
