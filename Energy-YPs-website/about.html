About EYP

Good conversations. Stronger connections.

Where we started

Energy Young Professionals began in Hamburg, Germany, in summer 2023. That community has grown to more than 200 people. The network expanded to Amsterdam in November 2024, where it has grown organically to around 80 members.

A network across the energy transition

Our members work across renewables, grids, finance, consulting, clean tech, mobility and related fields. Meeting people outside our everyday teams gives us a broader view of the sector and its practical challenges.

What brings us together

We aim to build a welcoming, engaged network where people know one another. Regular gatherings create space to exchange ideas, learn from experienced speakers and develop long-term professional relationships.

Who we are

Raphaël Sahakian

Founder and Director

Raphaël founded Energy Young Professionals in Hamburg in 2023 and launched the Amsterdam community in 2024. He leads EYP’s strategy, partnerships and events, drawing on his background in energy, sustainability and business development.

Simone Cerchiara

Partner

Simone brings a background in business consulting and the European energy transition. At EYP, he helps shape the community’s growth and leads work on professional development opportunities, including mentoring, coaching and workshops.
