About us

Good conversations. Stronger connections.

Energy Young Professionals (Energy YPs) brings together people from across the energy transition — from renewables and grids to finance, consulting, clean tech and mobility. Our goal is to build a welcoming, engaged network where people get to know each other beyond a single event.

Connect

Meet peers from different parts of the energy sector through relaxed, recurring gatherings.

Learn

Exchange ideas and hear practical perspectives from experienced industry speakers.

Grow together

Build long-term professional relationships within an active Amsterdam community.
